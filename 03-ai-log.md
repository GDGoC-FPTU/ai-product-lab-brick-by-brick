## 1. Toi da dung AI de lam gi?

Trong bai lab nay, toi su dung AI nhu mot cong cu brainstorm de tim kiem cac bai toan van hanh trong he sinh thai Vingroup. AI giup toi de xuat nhieu pain point thuoc cac cong ty nhu Vinhomes, VinFast, Vinpearl, Vinmec va Xanh SM, sau do sap xep chung theo cac lens nhu Lap lai, Ton thoi gian, AI-upgrade va Stakeholder Pain.

Sau khi co danh sach ban dau, toi tiep tuc dung AI de phan tich va lua chon 3 Quick Problem Cards phu hop nhat gom: Vinhomes tu dong phan loai khieu nai cu dan, VinFast tong hop bao cao bao hanh tu nhieu dai ly va Vinpearl phan tich review khach san. AI giup toi xac dinh actor, workflow hien tai, bottleneck, buoc AI co the tham gia, metric thanh cong va loai kien truc AI phu hop cho tung bai toan.

Ngoai ra, trong phan Deep-Dive cua nhom, toi su dung AI de tham khao cach mo ta workflow hien tai, workflow sau khi ung dung AI va cach xay dung cac operational boundary cho bai toan dieu phoi su co sac pin cua Xanh SM. Dieu nay giup nhom hoan thien report theo dung rubric cua buoi lab.

## 2. AI da sai, thieu, hoac gay hieu nham o dau?

Van de dau tien la AI thuong dua ra cac con so rat cu the nhu "giam 80% thoi gian", "dat do chinh xac 98%" hay "giam chi phi 30%". Tuy nhien, cac con so nay khong duoc xac minh bang du lieu noi bo cua doanh nghiep. Neu dua vao bao cao ma khong ghi ro day la uoc tinh thi de gay hieu nham.

Van de thu hai la AI doi luc de xuat su dung Agent cho nhieu bai toan don gian. Sau khi xem xet lai, toi thay phan lon bai toan trong Phase 1 va Phase 2 chi can LLM Feature de doc, tom tat va phan loai van ban, khong can Agent tu dong thuc hien hanh dong.

Ngoai ra, AI cung co xu huong bo qua Human-in-the-loop neu prompt khong noi ro. Dac biet doi voi bai toan cua nhom ve Xanh SM, AI khong duoc phep tu dong gui huong dan cho tai xe hoac tu quyet dinh phuong an cuu ho. Moi ket qua AI tao ra chi nen o dang draft de dispatcher xem va phe duyet.

## 3. Toi da sua prompt hoac kiem soat AI nhu the nao?

Toi dieu chinh prompt de AI tra loi dung theo cau truc Quick Problem Card thay vi tra loi dai dong. Moi lan tra loi deu yeu cau AI ghi ro bai toan, actor, workflow 3-5 buoc, bottleneck, buoc AI ho tro, metric va quick architecture.

Toi cung yeu cau AI phan biet ro giua Rule-based va LLM truoc khi dua ra giai phap. Neu mot cong viec chi can so sanh dieu kien, kiem tra quy tac hoac doi chieu du lieu thi uu tien Rule. Neu cong viec lien quan den ghi chu, review, van ban tu do hoac tai lieu dai thi moi su dung LLM.

Doi voi bai toan cua nhom ve Xanh SM, toi bo sung them cac operational boundary vao prompt de AI khong de xuat hanh dong vuot qua quyen han. AI chi duoc tao ban nhap huong dan, khong duoc gui truc tiep cho tai xe va khong duoc de xuat tram sac khong phu hop khi muc pin qua thap.

## 4. Bai hoc ca nhan

Qua bai lab nay, toi nhan ra AI rat huu ich trong giai doan brainstorming va phan tich bai toan van hanh. AI giup toi tim y tuong nhanh hon va de dang hinh dung duoc workflow hien tai cung nhu diem nghen cua quy trinh.

Tuy nhien, AI khong the thay the kien thuc nghiep vu. Tat ca cac metric, quy trinh va operational boundary deu can con nguoi xem xet va dieu chinh de dam bao phu hop voi thuc te. Neu chi tin vao cau tra loi cua AI ma khong kiem chung thi rat de dua ra nhung gia dinh khong chinh xac.

Bai hoc lon nhat toi rut ra la can bat dau tu bai toan kinh doanh truoc, sau do moi quyet dinh co can AI hay khong. Nhieu quy trinh co the giai quyet bang Rule-based hoac cai tien workflow, trong khi LLM chi nen duoc su dung o cac buoc xu ly ngon ngu tu nhien nhu tom tat, phan loai va sinh noi dung ban nhap. Ngoai ra, voi cac bai toan anh huong den van hanh thuc te nhu Xanh SM, Human-in-the-loop luon la yeu cau bat buoc de dam bao an toan va giam rui ro.
